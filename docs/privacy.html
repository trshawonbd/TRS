Who we are

Deshi Diet Thala ("the app") is a free recipe and family cooking app at trshawonbd.github.io/TRS. It is run by an individual developer, not a company. Questions: github.com/trshawonbd/TRS/issues.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google. We do not receive your Google password or access to your Gmail, contacts or files.
  • What you add to the app: your family's name and members, favourite recipes, today's dish, ingredients marked as "have" or "need", shopping list items, messages and reminders you send, dishes you mark as cooked, and photos of dishes you upload.
  • Notifications: if you turn notifications on, your browser gives us a technical address used only to deliver notifications to that device.
  • On your device only: your "My diet" details (sex, age, height, weight, goal weight, activity level, calorie target, fasting schedule and weight log), settings such as language, theme and appliance choices, and, if you use the app without an account, your favourites and cooking log. These stay in your browser and are never sent to us or shown to your family.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not collect your location.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To share your family's recipes, shopping list, messages, photos and cooking progress with the members of your family, and with no one else.
  • To send the notifications you asked for.
  • To show anonymous totals, such as "12 families cooked this recipe". These show only a number, never who.

We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

Only you and the people who joined your family with your invite code. Photos are stored privately and are visible only to your family.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase stores accounts, family data and photos, in its EU (Frankfurt) region.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts.
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google or Apple), only if you turn notifications on.

Like any website, these services may see your IP address when your device connects to them.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. You can remove your own photos and cooking entries in the app at any time. To delete your whole account and its data, ask us at the link above and we will delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your information at any time. If you live in the EU or UK, you also have the rights given by the GDPR, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The app is meant for adults planning family meals. It is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Health note

Recipes and calorie numbers are general estimates, not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
